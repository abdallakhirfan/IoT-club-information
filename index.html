Who We Are

A community for students who want to build, not just study.

The Internet of Things Club was founded in 2024 at PSUT to give students a place to explore IoT beyond the lecture hall — sensors, microcontrollers, wireless networks, and the smart systems that connect them.

We bring together students from Communications Engineering, Computer Engineering, and anyone curious about how physical devices talk to each other and to the cloud. No prior experience is required — just curiosity and a willingness to build things that actually work.

Mission & Objectives

Closing the gap between the classroom and the industry.

Our Mission

To equip PSUT students with practical, real-world skills in the Internet of Things — supporting their success both in their studies and in their careers.

Run hands-on workshops on IoT tools, embedded systems, and sensor networks.
Organize competitions that challenge students to solve real IoT problems.
Host talks and discussions with engineers and industry leaders in IoT.
Open doors to training programs and job opportunities in the IoT field.
Build a community of students who learn by building things together.
Represent PSUT at IoT events and workshops with other universities.
What We Do

Three ways we get students building.

Everything the club does is built around learning by doing.

Workshops

Practical sessions on Arduino, ESP32, sensors, and the software tools behind connected devices — built for beginners and made to be repeated.

Competitions

Team challenges on real IoT problems, from smart-home prototypes to sensor networks — a chance to test what a workshop taught.

Industry Talks

Conversations with IoT engineers and founders, giving members a clearer view of where the field is heading and how to break into it.
